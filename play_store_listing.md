## Full Description
🕉️ **Divine-Earthly: Sovereign AI for Indian Farmers**

Built entirely on a mobile phone in Silchar, Assam — now serving farmers across India.

### What This App Does
- 🌾 **Crop Intelligence:** Get personalized crop recommendations based on your soil type, weather, and moon phase
- 🦠 **Disease Detection:** Identify 40+ crop diseases and get organic Panchgavya treatments
- 💰 **Mandi Prices:** Check real-time market prices and MSP for your crops
- 🛡️ **Water Safety:** Test drinking water quality with BIS 10500:2012 compliance
- 🛰️ **Satellite View:** See your fields from space (Sentinel-2 satellite)
- 🌦️ **7-Day Forecast:** Know exactly when to sow, irrigate, or harvest
- 🗣️ **Voice Commands:** Speak in Hindi, Bengali, or Assamese
- 📵 **100% Offline:** Works without internet after first data download
