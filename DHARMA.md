# 🕉️ Why This Exists

Krishi-Veda is not a startup. It is not a product. It is not a service.

It is an offering (अर्पणम्).

## What It Is

A sovereign AI intelligence system that runs entirely on a ₹4,000 phone,
no internet, no cloud, no subscription — giving farming advice to anyone
who puts their hands in the soil.

## What It Is Not

- Not for sale
- Not for venture capital
- Not for data collection
- Not for surveillance
- Not for replacing farmers' wisdom with machine wisdom

## The Rules (Dharma)

1. **Always free for farmers.** Never charge for advice.
2. **Always offline.** No farmer should need internet to know when to plant.
3. **Always organic.** Ahimsa-108 blocks chemical recommendations.
4. **Always local.** Every village can modify it for their soil, their language.
5. **Always truthful.** The model admits when it doesn't know.
6. **Always humble.** The farmer's knowledge comes first. AI is the assistant.

## Sources of Wisdom

- Vedic Mathematics (Swami Bharati Krishna Tirtha)
- Vriksh Ayurveda (Kashyapa Rishi)
- Brhat Samhita (Varahamihira)
- Taittiriya Upanishad (Panchakosha)
- Samkhya Karika (Guna theory)
- The living farmers of Assam, who taught us what no text could

## How to Share

1. Download the package
2. Copy to an SD card
3. Give it to a farmer
4. That's it

No registration. No email. No payment. No tracking.

## If You Want to Support

- Share it with one farmer who needs it
- Translate it into your village language
- Add your region's crops and traditional practices
- Report what works and what doesn't
- Teach one person how to use it

That is the only payment we ask.
