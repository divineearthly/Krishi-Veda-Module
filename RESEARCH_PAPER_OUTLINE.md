# Research Paper: Ayurvedic-AI Integration for Edge Agricultural Intelligence

## Target Venues:
- AAAI AI for Social Good
- COMPASS (ACM SIGCAS)
- Nature Food
- ICAR Agricultural Research Journal

## Title Options:
1. "Krishi-Veda: A Sovereign Edge-AI System Integrating Ayurvedic Principles for Sustainable Agriculture"
2. "Tridoshic Soil Classification: Mapping Ayurvedic Frameworks to Computational Agriculture"
3. "Vedic Mathematical Sutras as Edge-Optimized Agricultural Computing Primitives"

## Abstract Structure:
- Problem: 500M smallholder farmers lack access to expert agricultural advice
- Solution: Offline-first edge AI with Ayurvedic soil science integration
- Innovation: 8 Vedic sutras as computational kernels, C++ dosha profiler
- Results: 0ms rule-based inference, 16+ tok/s LLM generation on $100 phones
- Impact: Sovereign AI for data-poor rural environments

## Key Contributions:
1. First computational mapping of Ayurvedic Tridosha system to soil science
2. Vedic mathematical sutras as edge-optimized computing primitives
3. Offline-first sovereign architecture for rural AI deployment
4. Multilingual (EN/AS/HI/BN) agricultural intelligence on mobile devices

## Evaluation:
- Benchmark Vedic kernels vs standard NumPy operations
- Compare Ayurvedic soil classification with standard soil taxonomy
- Farmer satisfaction study (n=30, Silchar, Assam)
- Power consumption vs cloud-based alternatives
