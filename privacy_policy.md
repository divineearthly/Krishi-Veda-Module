# Privacy Policy — Divine-Earthly

**Last Updated: May 2026**

## Our Commitment
Divine-Earthly is built on the principle of **Sovereign AI** — your data stays on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information to any server.

## Data We DO NOT Collect
- Personal identification (name, email, phone)
- Location data (GPS stays on device)
- Farm data (soil, crops, yields stay on device)
- Voice recordings (processed offline via Vosk)
- Camera images (processed locally for disease detection)

## Data Stored Locally
- Cached weather data for offline use
- Soil health history (on your device only)
- App preferences (language, units)

## Internet Access
The app uses internet ONLY for:
- Fetching weather forecasts (Open-Meteo, no API key)
- Satellite imagery (Google Earth Engine, authenticated)
- Downloading updates from GitHub

No personal data is transmitted during these operations.

## Third Parties
- Google Earth Engine: Used for satellite data. No personal data shared.
- Open-Meteo: Used for weather. No personal data shared.
- Hugging Face: Hosts backend API. No user accounts required.
