# 🌳 Vriksh Ayurveda Agricultural Kernels

## Ancient Indian Plant Science in Modern AI

Vriksh Ayurveda is the ancient Indian science of plant life and agriculture, primarily documented in:
- **Kashyapiyakrishisukti** (Kashyapa's Agricultural Treatise, ~800 BCE)
- **Vriksh Ayurveda** (The Science of Plant Life, attributed to Parasara)
- **Brhat Samhita** (Varahamihira, 6th century CE)
- **Arthashastra** (Kautilya, 4th century BCE — Book 2, Chapter 24 on Agriculture)
